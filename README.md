# Wedding RSVP

A mobile-first RSVP page for Ashrith & Aishwarya's wedding (17–19 Nov 2026, Nature Knots Resort, Bengaluru), served by GitHub Pages from the root of `main`.

- Answers are saved to a private Google Sheet. See [`apps-script/SETUP.md`](apps-script/SETUP.md) for the one-time setup, then paste the web-app URL into [`config.js`](config.js).
- The 17 Nov Mehendi & Sangeet date is only shown to guests who say they'll dance.
- The accommodation and travel questions only appear for guests with us across both days (all of 18 & 19 Nov, or an 18th event plus the wedding on the 19th).
- Each phone number gets one row in the sheet. Re-submitting updates that row, and every version is kept in the History tab.

Live at https://ashrith333.github.io/wedding/
