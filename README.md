# Wedding RSVP

A mobile-first RSVP page for Ashrith & Aishwarya's wedding (17 to 19 Nov 2026, Nature Knots Resort, Bengaluru), served by GitHub Pages from the root of `main`.

Live at https://ashrith333.github.io/wedding/

- Answers are saved to a private Google Sheet. See [`apps-script/SETUP.md`](apps-script/SETUP.md) for the one-time setup. The web-app URL lives in [`config.js`](config.js).
- The 17 Nov Mehendi & Sangeet date is only shown to guests who say they'll dance.
- Accommodation is asked only when the chosen celebrations span more than one day. Guests can pick 1 night, or 2 nights when they're with us from the 17th to the 19th.
- Each phone number gets one row in the sheet. Re-submitting updates that row, and every version is kept in the History tab.
